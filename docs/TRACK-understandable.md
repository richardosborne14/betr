> *"Can we write up a bunch of tasks to tackle the problems these users faced and make the

## The one finding underneath all of them

Every moment in the three walks that worked **described the inside of a moment**. Every moment
that nearly lost somebody **named a kind of person**. That is B22, and it is the rule the other
five answer to — including the two that are about where a required category *sits* rather than
whether it stays.

## The order I would take them in

```

DONE       DONE               the rule    the doors   the number   Help
```

**B24 was first**, because it was the only one that was a harm rather than a loss: a person told

**B27's first three are done**, 2026-09-04, and they took the hour they were costed at. The miss

**So B22 is next**, and it is where it always was in this order.

**B22 now**, because B23, B25 and B26 are all easier to decide once the piles exist and everyone
is looking at the same table.

## What none of these tasks may do

- **Add a screen.** Rule 10. Six taps is the shape; B19 and B20 each bought one knowingly.
- **Change a frozen sentence, a crisis line or a store requirement.** B22 says where a required
  category belongs, never whether it stays.
- **Delete anything for being intimidating.** The heavy words in Help are all correct. The
  question everywhere is what is met first.
- **Treat the three walks as evidence about real people.** Three characters written by the author
  of the app catch what fails on sight and nothing subtler. The CBT reviewer, Misha's read of the
  doors, and a real screen-reader pass are all still release conditions and none of them is this.
