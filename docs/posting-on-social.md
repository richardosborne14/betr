# Posting on social — a guide for someone who doesn't do social

> Written 2026-09-10 for the founder and Misha. This is the "how", not the "whether".
> The strategy sits in [`tasks/B7-distribution-and-the-ad.md`](tasks/B7-distribution-and-the-ad.md);
> the sequencing argument (why B6's gate matters first) is in that task and in the session notes.
> Nothing here changes the app, and nothing here needs a developer.

---

## 0. The one idea to hold on to

**You are not posting about an app. You are posting a filled-in worksheet.**

The technical instinct is to explain the product — what it does, why it's private, how it's
built. That is the post that gets removed, ignored, or answered with "another AI app".

[B7](tasks/B7-distribution-and-the-ad.md) already decided the creative and it is one line:
**the result screen is the ad.** A prediction with a line through it, what actually happened
underneath, and a number coming down. No face, no voice, no claim.

The useful test before you post anything:

> **Would this sentence be allowed on the App Store listing?**

If no, it can't go on social either — rule 7 means the purpose statement and the claims are
identical in the app, the listing and *every post*. Research §5.4's worked example from the
MHRA is precisely a company that said "well-being" on its site and something stronger on
social. That company had to regulate the product or delete the claim.

## 1. Setting up the accounts

### Which ones

| Platform | Worth it? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Instagram** | **Yes** | Carousels and Reels. Lowest social obligation of the lot. |
| **TikTok** | **Yes** | Photo carousels work fine — you do not need to film anything. Most reach per unit of effort. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | **Yes, free** | Same vertical asset, re-uploaded. Near-zero comment culture. Do it because it costs one drag-and-drop. |
| **Reddit** | **Yes, but not as BETR** | See §6. This is you, personally, as a member. A brand account gets removed. |
| **X / Twitter** | **No** | Reply-driven, needs daily presence, rewards argument. Wrong shape for you and for this. |
| **Facebook** | **No** | The audience for this isn't there, and Groups need constant moderation. |

Use the **same handle everywhere**. Don't get clever with spelling — people will type what they
heard.

### The bio, and a real problem with it

Rule 7 freezes the purpose statement. It reads:

> BETR helps you test unhelpful beliefs in everyday life. You pick a worry about how people
> will react, it gives you one small thing to try today, and you record what actually happened.

That is 194 characters. **Instagram's bio limit is 150. TikTok's is 80.** It does not fit, and
paraphrasing it to fit is exactly the thing rule 7 exists to stop.

**The way through** — and this is a founder decision, not mine to take:

- **Bio** = the first sentence, word for word, nothing added:
  `BETR helps you test unhelpful beliefs in everyday life.` (55 characters, fits everywhere.)
  A literal substring makes no new claim.
- **Pinned first post** = the whole purpose statement, plus frozen sentences 1, 8 and 9.
- **Link** = `https://betr.trybeup.com` — plain, no parameter. Same rule as inside the app.

⚠️ **Blocker to clear first.** The purpose statement still describes the old way in ("you pick a
worry about how people will react") — [`00-scope.md` §35](00-scope.md) flags it, and
[B29](tasks/B29-the-rules-and-the-word.md) has a candidate rewrite waiting on the founder.
Right now it is frozen in five places. **The day you open a bio it is a sixth.** Fix the
sentence before there are posts carrying it, not after.

### What does not go in the bio

- No "mental health", no "anxiety", no condition names — rule 7 bans "for people with
  [diagnosis]" and a bio is the most listing-like thing on the account.
- No "join our community", no "DM me", no "link in bio 👇".
- No mention of TrybeUP beyond the lineage line. Rule 9.
- No follower count games, no "1k and I'll post X".

**Do keep the lineage visible.** "Made by the people behind TrybeUP" in the pinned post. If
people work out later that BETR funnels to TrybeUP, having said it up front is the entire
difference between honest and sneaky. Research 08 is full of people who felt sold to.

## 2. The four post formats

Everything you will ever post is one of these four. Make them in batches.

### Format A — the result card ★ the main one

The screenshot [B7](tasks/B7-distribution-and-the-ad.md) describes. One test, finished.

```
  You expected
  ────────────────────────────────
  T̶h̶e̶y̶'̶l̶l̶ ̶s̶p̶o̶t̶ ̶t̶h̶e̶ ̶r̶o̶u̶g̶h̶ ̶e̶d̶g̶e̶s̶ ̶a̶n̶d̶ ̶q̶u̶i̶e̶t̶l̶y̶
  d̶e̶c̶i̶d̶e̶ ̶I̶'̶v̶e̶ ̶d̶r̶o̶p̶p̶e̶d̶ ̶o̶f̶f̶.̶

  What actually happened
  ────────────────────────────────
  Nobody said anything. One person said
  thanks.

  How sure you are it goes badly
  Started 8  →  Now 5
```

**Caption:** nothing at all, or one flat line. `Handed it over at good enough. Day four.`

That's the whole post. The restraint *is* the advertising.

### Format B — the two-parter

Post the prediction on day one, the result on day two. It is the only format with any
suspense in it, and it costs nothing extra.

- **Day 1:** `If I hand over this at good enough, then it'll come straight back to me with a list.`
- **Day 2:** the result card for the same test.

### Format C — the plain sentence card

White or near-black background, one sentence, nothing else. Written to the state a person is
actually in.

- `You already know what you think will happen. You've never checked.`
- `The prediction is doing all the work, and it's never been written down.`
- `Not "stop worrying". Just find out whether you were right.`

⚠️ **Write these fresh.** The language bank in
[`research/08-participant-voice-recovery.md`](research/08-participant-voice-recovery.md) §6 is
the right register to aim at — *numb, tired, the revolving door, nobody in my real life knows* —
but **do not lift the verbatim quotes.** Those are real people's posts, and putting one in an ad
is a different act from quoting it in research. Write your own line in the same register.

⚠️ And note what that section found: **"become the man you want to be" appears in 61 of 128,656
posts.** It is TrybeUP's positioning and it is one of the rarest phrasings in the corpus. Do not
carry it over. Write to the state somebody is in, never the identity they should want.

### Format D — the explainer, once

Pinned, and posted maybe once a month. What the thing actually is, in the frozen words:

> It uses one technique from cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), the behavioural experiment:
> write down a belief, predict what will happen, try it, record what actually happened, and
> rate the belief again.

Plus: free, no account, works offline, nothing leaves your phone, no AI.

**Say "no AI" explicitly and early.** Research §9.3: the one free-app post that got removed drew
"Another AI post" as its *first comment*. It is the assumption now. Get in front of it.

## 4. Actually making the image

You do not need Canva, a designer, or a subscription.

**Easiest route, and the most honest one:** open `https://betr.trybeup.com` on your phone, run a
real test, and **screenshot the result screen**. That is the ad. It is real, it is already
designed, and it is already on-brand because it *is* the brand.

- Use your own tests, or made-up ones. **Never anyone else's, even anonymised.**
- Crop out the status bar (battery, carrier) — it dates the post and looks careless.

**Sizes:**

| Where | Size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram feed | 1080 × 1350 | The tall one. Use this, not the square. |
| Reels / TikTok / Shorts | 1080 × 1920 | Same asset, more padding top and bottom. |
| Stories | 1080 × 1920 | Keep text out of the top and bottom 250px. |

**"Video" without filming anything.** Both TikTok and Instagram accept **photo carousels**, and
they perform fine. A three-card carousel — prediction, then what happened, then the ladder — is
a complete post and requires no video editing whatsoever. If you want actual motion, a 6-second
screen recording of the result screen is enough. **Nobody needs to see or hear you.**

## 5. How often, and how to do it without living there

**Three posts a week. That is plenty and more is worse.**

The trick for someone who doesn't want to be on these platforms: **batch and schedule.**

1. One afternoon a month, make twelve posts.
2. Schedule all twelve. Instagram (via Meta Business Suite) and TikTok both have a built-in
   scheduler. Free. No third-party tool, no extra account.
3. Then open the apps **twice a week for fifteen minutes** to handle comments (§7).

That is the entire time commitment: one afternoon plus two short check-ins a week.

**First four weeks:**

| Week | Mon | Wed | Fri |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **D** — the explainer, pinned | A — post 1 | C — post 6 |
| 2 | B — post 4, part one | B — post 4, part two | A — post 2 |
| 3 | C — post 7 | A — post 3 | B — post 5, part one |
| 4 | B — post 5, part two | A — new one of your own | C — new one of your own |

## 6. Reddit is different, and it is the valuable one

[B7](tasks/B7-distribution-and-the-ad.md) has this mapped and the rules are strict because the
communities enforce them. **Do not point the BETR account at Reddit.** Brand accounts get
removed on sight.

What research §9.3 found, plainly: r/stopdrinking has "zero tolerance for any kind of personal
or commercial promotion" and removed a 138-upvote post. r/NoFap removed at least nine "I built
an app" posts in five months. r/socialanxiety removed one after fourteen months of work.

**The survivors were free, story-first, from a member, no paywall, no AI.**

So:

1. **Be a member for two weeks first.** Actually read, actually reply, on your own account.
   This is not a growth hack, it is the rule those subs enforce and they can see your history.
2. **Reply, don't post.** r/CBT has a May 2025 request for a behavioural-experiment worksheet
   app sitting there with **zero replies**. That is the opening.
3. **Read each sub's rules live before each post** — the rule text for several of them couldn't
   be fetched during research.
4. **No upsell to TrybeUP in the post at all.** The lineage line is on the page, not in the
   pitch.

## 7. The conventions nobody writes down

This is the part that isn't obvious from knowing the technology.

**Reply to comments, briefly, within a few hours.** Platforms weight early replies and it's how
a post travels. Two sentences maximum. You are not obliged to be warm; you are obliged to be
straight.

**When someone says "this is just CBT" or "another AI app":** answer once, flat, and stop.
`No AI in it. None.` Do not argue, do not write a paragraph, do not reply twice. Arguing in a
comment section has never once helped anybody's product.

🚨 **Someone will comment in crisis. Decide what you do before it happens.**

This is not hypothetical for an account in this space, and [B7](tasks/B7-distribution-and-the-ad.md)
does not cover it. Save frozen sentence 7 as a note on your phone and paste it, unedited:

> If you are in danger or in crisis, call your local emergency number. In the US, call or text
> 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans free on 116 123. Elsewhere, findahelpline.com
> lists free helplines in over 175 countries.

Then stop. **Do not improvise, do not counsel, do not move it to DMs.** Frozen sentence 9 says
using BETR does not create a therapist–client relationship — behaving like a therapist in a
comment section is how that stops being true.

**Turn DMs off, or set an auto-reply pointing at the Help screen.** You must not become a
support service by accident. This is a real exposure, not squeamishness.

**Never react to a personal disclosure with a heart or a like.** Someone posting something
painful under an ad and getting a ❤️ from the advertiser reads as harvesting. Reply in words or
not at all.

**Don't follow-for-follow, don't buy followers, don't comment-bomb other accounts.** All three
are visible, all three are read as desperation, and this product's only asset is being trusted.

## 8. The never-do list

- ❌ Never claim it works. Lumosity paid $2M for efficacy claims without evidence (research §5.7).
- ❌ Never use: "digital CBT", "treats", "reduces symptoms", "for people with [diagnosis]",
  "tracks your anxiety", "improve your mental health". Not once, not in a hashtag, not in a
  reply.
- ❌ **Never a condition hashtag.** `#anxiety` `#socialanxiety` `#depression` edge straight into
  "for people with [diagnosis]". Tag the *action*, not the *condition* — or skip hashtags
  entirely, which on current Instagram and TikTok costs you almost nothing.
- ❌ Never post a real person's test.
- ❌ Never quote the research corpus verbatim.
- ❌ Never a "before / after" mental-health framing.
- ❌ Never mention TrybeUP outside the lineage line.
- ❌ Never the word "AI", except to say there isn't any.
- ❌ Never a streak, a score, or a "day 30" — rule 5 applies to the marketing as much as the app.

## 9. What you can count

- **Outbound link clicks** — Instagram and TikTok both report these natively. This is your
  social → BETR number and it costs nothing and breaks nothing.
- **Store downloads**, once B5 ships.
- **Replies and people who write to the "tell us" address.**

**Nothing else, by design.** The BETR → TrybeUP conversion number is
[B6](tasks/B6-the-trybeup-bridge.md)'s `signup_source_type = betr`, which only exists once that
gate opens. There is no shortcut to it and no parameter that gets you there sooner.
